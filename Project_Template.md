4. How would a business or a community (for Social Impact Projects) benefit from your work?
> Be specific. Think money saved, process improves, better decisions made
> That’s your real value

- saves money: fuel and vehicle maintenance are the highest operational costs for wildlife reserves. Instead of driving aimless, predictable paths, rangers can deploy directly to high-probability sectors. This saves money in fuel while increasing operational presence.

- Helps saves species:  By identifying hotspot trends before a patrol leaves base, rangers can clear illegal wire snares prior to animals getting trapped. This directly protects biodiversity and endangered species.

- Eco-Tourism & Local Community Economy: Many rural communities rely entirely on eco-tourism driven by healthy wildlife populations. Protecting these animals stabilizes the local economy, secures tourism jobs, and funds community schools or health clinics adjacent to the reserve.
